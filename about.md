<h2>Enterprise Technologist. Systems Architect. Digital Strategist.</h2>

<p>For over two decades, I have operated at the intersection of enterprise web architecture, human-centered design, and institutional governance. My focus has always centered on how humans inhabit digital spaces—from early interactive multimedia to modern, AI-augmented digital ecosystems. As a technical lead managing a portfolio of over 300 enterprise websites, I specialize in architecting scalable solutions under complex institutional constraints. When federal accessibility mandates required comprehensive compliance, I led the technical strategy—engineering centralized remediation code, authoring standards documentation, and briefing executive leadership to build an institutional culture of accessibility. Backed by a Master of Science in the Management of IT from the McIntire School of Commerce at the University of Virginia, I help organizations navigate technical transformations with clarity. I combine hands-on engineering depth with executive strategy to ensure technology serves human needs responsibly, ethically, and at scale.</p>
